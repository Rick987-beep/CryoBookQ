Methodology notes

Universe
BTC options only. Exact match on (expiry, strike, call/put). Unmatched listings are excluded from landmark cells.
$10k lift
Walk the ask until ~$10 000 of option premium is filled (not spot BTC). Score uses (VWAP − mid) / mid.
0–10 maps
Absolute reference bands per delta bucket (documented in CryoBookQ SCORING.md). Missing/one-sided → 0 for that component. Each cell blends 65% relative spread, 25% $10k lift, 10% L5 depth.
Period scores
When multiple snapshots are included, each snapshot gets a full scorecard; reported figures are equal-weight averages across snapshots.
Deltas
Canonical |Δ| from Deribit mark IV / forward (Black–Scholes), attached to both venues’ matched contracts for landmark selection.
